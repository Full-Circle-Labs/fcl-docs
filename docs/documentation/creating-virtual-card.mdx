# Virtual Cards

## Create a virtual card

Once logged into your account, click on the account icon (<img src="/img/icon-account.png" alt="account icon" style={{width: '3%'}} />) and select Virtual Cards in the drop down menu. 
You will be redirected to the Virtual Cards management dashboard (Figure 1). 
<div style={{textAlign: 'center'}}>
<img src="/img/virtual-card-management-dashboard.png" alt="Virtual Cards management dashboard" style={{width: '70%'}} />

*Figure 1 - Virtual Cards management dashboard*
</div>

Click the "+" to create a new card. You will be asked to give a name to your card, if you don't give one, a default code will be attributed.
Your new virtual card will now appear on the dashboard.
<div style={{textAlign: 'center'}}>
<img src="/img/virtual-card-management-dashboard-3.png" alt="Virtual Cards management dashboard with one card listed" style={{width: '70%'}} />

*Figure 2 - Virtual Cards management dashboard with one virtual card*
</div>

If you have multiple cards, you might want to select one as default. 
To do this, select the card you want to set as default and click on the star. Once your card is set as default, the star will appear as solid.

## Top up a virtual card

To use a virtual card, you will need to top it up. From the Virtual Cards management dashboard, select the virtual card you want to top up and press the "Top up" button.
You will be asked for the amount you want to add to your card then you will be able to checkout. 
Virtual cards can only be topped up using credit card or a PO, please have a look at our [checkout guide](/docs/documentation/orders#checkout). 


## Share a virtual card

Sometimes several people are involved in big projects, to make it easier to track the budget of a project, we have developed a feature that allows you to share your virtual cards. To share your virtual card, click on "Users", 
fill in the account email you want to share the card with then click "Share this card"

**Example:** \
The project lead can create a virtual card, top it up then share it with different team members involved in the project, the different users of the virtual card will be listed under "Users" and all the transactions performed on the virtual card under "Transactions".\

## Download the transactions from a virtual card
For reporting purposes, you can also download a `.csv` file containing the list of the transactions performed with one virtual card. 

## Generate a quote
If you need a quote to generate a PO for topping up a virtual card, click on "Generate quote", enter the amount and then "Generate quote".
