# Orders

## Add samples to your cart
Login to your account on our website, on the main page, click the "Purchase" button next to the product you would like to order. You will be redirected to the sample submission form.
Click "Add sample" to add the number of samples you want and fill in your sample names following the instructions there. After filling in your sample information, you can either press "Save draft" to continue your order later, "Clear" to empty your cart, or "Go to checkout" to finalise your order.

:::info
You can only have one cart at a time so selecting a new product from the main page will overwrite your current cart. Make sure to finalise your order before adding other products to your order.

:::

## Checkout

Checkout can be done using one of the three following options:

- Virtual Balance
    - Select the Virtual card you'd like to use
    - Click Place order
- Credit card, to pay with credit card you need to provide a shipping address:
    - Fill in your credit card information
    - Click Place order
- Pay with PO:
    - Fill in your PO number
    - Click Place order
    - Send your PO by email to contact@fullcirclelabs.bio

## Submit samples


1. **Transfer the recommended sample volume in PCR strips/plates.**\
   *At least 10 µL for plasmids and amplicons, 20 µL for libraries.*

2. **Label your samples as described below:**\
   a. Write the sample number on one side of the tubes.\
   b. Write the order number on the first and last sample on the other side of the tubes.\
      *For operational reasons we sometimes split PCR strips in half, having the order number on both ends helps us keep track of your order.*

   <img src="/img/tube_annotation_left.svg" alt="Tube labelling diagram (first half)" style={{filter: 'var(--img-invert)', width: '30%'}}/>
   <img src="/img/tube_annotation_right.svg" alt="Tube labelling diagram (second half)" style={{filter: 'var(--img-invert)', width: '30%'}}/>

3. **Place your samples in a reusable bag or another sealed package (sealable plastic bags or Falcon tubes work well).**\
   *If you are not using a reusable bag, make sure to write the order number of the package.*



:::info 
Interested in using our reusable bags? Reach out to [contact@fullcirclelabs.bio](mailto:contact@fullcirclelabs.bio).
:::
:::warning
- Do not use 0.5 or 1.5 ml tubes, transferring samples into PCR strips causes delay and risk of sample mismatch.
- Do not write the sample name on the tubes, it's difficult for us to read and this can cause delays.
- Do not wrap tubes in parafilm or tape the bag, this causes delays and makes it more difficult to recycle the tubes.
- If your lab doesn't have suitable tubes you can reach out to us and we will try to provide you with some.
:::
*If we notice a missing sample, missing labels or receive samples in 0.5 or 1.5 ml tubes, you will get a notification by email. We try to not delay the processing of your samples and so you may still receive results even if you receive these notifications.*


## Check an order status

Click "Orders" in the top menu. 
Since our turnaround times are so quick we generally only mark items as "Ordered" or "Completed", but are introducing intermediate statuses like "Samples Arrived" and "Sequencing". 
To take advantage of our "Samples Arrived" sample status please use our new RFID sample bags.
