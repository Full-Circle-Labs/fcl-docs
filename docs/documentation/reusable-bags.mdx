FCL uses reusable sample bags to reduce packaging waste. Learn more in our [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7281992304624644096).

 Each bag has a unique bag code and QR code. Before submitting your order you need to link your bag to it, here's how depending on how you're placing your order:

### On the Website

1. Place your samples, in PCR strips/plates, into the bag
2. Sign In on your account
3. Choose the product
4. Create your sample list
5. At checkout, enter your bag code in the 'FCL bag code' box and double-check it is correct (see the image below)
6. Checkout
7. Drop your samples in the nearest drop box, or mail them to us

### On Smartphone

1. Place your samples, in PCR strips/plates, into the bag
2. Scan the QR code
3. Sign in to your account, the bag code will be pre-filled on your order
4. Choose the product
5. Create your sample list
6. At checkout, double-check that your bag code is correct
7. Checkout
8. Drop your samples in the nearest drop box, or mail them to us
